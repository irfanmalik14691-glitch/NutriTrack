A. Healthy Weight Gain

Lean Hypertrophy & Caloric Surplus
+300 to 500 kcal

1. The Calorie Surplus Science

To build new muscle fibers, your body requires positive energy balance. A controlled surplus of 300–500 kcal/day above your TDEE yields approximately 0.25 to 0.5 kg of lean mass per week. Avoid "dirty bulking" (uncontrolled surplus >1000 kcal) which stores excess energy as visceral fat.

2. Macronutrient Distribution

  • Protein (1.8g – 2.2g per kg): Provides amino acids for muscle protein synthesis.
  • Carbohydrates (50–55% of calories): Replenishes muscle glycogen to fuel heavy training sets.
  • Healthy Fats (20–25% of calories): Crucial for natural testosterone and hormone production.

3. Affordable Indian High-Calorie Meals

• High-Calorie Desi Power Shake: 60g Rolled Oats, 300ml whole milk, 2 tbsp natural peanut butter, 1 banana, 2 tbsp sattu or 1 scoop whey (~750 kcal, 38g protein).
• Paneer Bhurji with Paratha/Roti: 150g fresh paneer cooked in 1 tsp mustard oil with onions, tomatoes, and 3 whole wheat rotis (~680 kcal, 32g protein).
• Soya Chunks & Egg Pulao: 50g raw soya chunks boiled and stir-fried with 1 cup cooked basmati rice and 2 whole boiled eggs (~560 kcal, 38g protein).
• Evening Snack: Handful of roasted almonds (20g) + 2 bananas (~320 kcal).

4. Muscle-Building Essentials

Progressive Overload: You must gradually increase weight, repetitions, or improve movement quality each week.
Sleep: 7.5 to 8.5 hours of uninterrupted sleep is mandatory for growth hormone release and muscle repair.

B. Safe Fat Loss

Muscle Sparing & Caloric Deficit
-400 to 500 kcal

1. The Safe Deficit Principle

To lose stored adipose tissue while protecting muscle mass, maintain a moderate deficit of 400–500 kcal/day below TDEE. This yields 0.4 to 0.7 kg fat loss per week without triggering metabolic slowdown, ravenous cravings, or muscle loss.

2. Elevated Protein Strategy

Consume 1.8g to 2.0g protein per kg of body weight. Protein has the highest Thermic Effect of Food (TEF) consuming 25% of its calories simply in digestion, suppresses ghrelin (hunger hormone), and protects lean muscle tissue.

3. Nutritious Indian Cutting Meals

• Sprouted Moong Salad: 150g boiled moong sprouts, chopped cucumber, tomato, green chilies, lemon juice, and roasted cumin (~180 kcal, 14g protein, high fiber).
• Egg White Scramble & Roti: 5 egg whites + 1 whole egg scrambled with veggies, served with 2 plain rotis and cucumber raita (~380 kcal, 28g protein).
• Grilled Chicken Breast / Low Fat Paneer: 150g marinated in curd and tandoori spices, air-fried or pan-grilled (~240 kcal, 38g protein).
• Satiety Hack: Drink 500ml water 15 minutes before lunch and dinner.

4. Strength Training + NEAT

Lift weights 3–4 days per week with heavy compound exercises so your body preserves muscle. Combine with 8,000–10,000 daily steps (NEAT) for low-fatigue fat burning rather than excessive exhausting cardio.
