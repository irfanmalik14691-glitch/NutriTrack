Scientific Nutrition Formulation Notice

NutriTrack uses the clinically validated Mifflin-St Jeor formula:
Men: BMR = (10 × weight[kg]) + (6.25 × height[cm]) - (5 × age) + 5
Women: BMR = (10 × weight[kg]) + (6.25 × height[cm]) - (5 × age) - 161

Scientific Disclaimer: All BMR, TDEE, and macronutrient targets calculated by NutriTrack are scientific estimates designed for healthy athletic adults. Individual basal metabolism varies with genetics, thyroid function, and non-exercise activity thermogenesis (NEAT). Consult a licensed sports dietician or physician before beginning any rapid caloric alteration.
